MainWindow — top-level application window with full signal/slot wiring.

QMainWindow with QSplitter (65/35), bottom QDockWidget, menu bar, toolbar, InspectorPanel, AnalysisControlBar, ResultsDockPanel, and NotificationRail. All actions, keyboard shortcuts, and dirty-state guards are wired through MainWindowController.
